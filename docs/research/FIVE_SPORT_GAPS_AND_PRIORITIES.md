# Five-sport evidence, measurement gaps and priorities

**Working factor map, 2026-10-11. See the [final research synthesis](FIVE_SPORT_RESEARCH_SYNTHESIS.md) for the approved staged build decision.** No tactical model
is fitted or evaluated by this document. The sport-specific literature reviews
are still receiving focused follow-up, particularly on biomechanics and measurement
alternatives. The completed four-sport match forecasting release remains a separate,
preserved result.

## What the broader research must answer

The goal is to explain and test meaningful sports context, including positioning
and mechanics where actual measurements exist. Availability alone does not decide
which mechanisms matter. Conversely, a plausible mechanism or a published private-
data study does not supply measurements for our own model.

The evidence map therefore separates four questions for each factor:

1. What physical or tactical mechanism is proposed, and what supports it?
2. What was actually measured, in whom, under what conditions?
3. Which of those measurements are available and usable in this project?
4. What claim can the proposed analysis identify or test, with what uncertainty?

The following common taxonomy prevents a source's convenient columns from defining
the entire research question. It is a coverage framework, not a claim that every
factor is measured or that every mechanism has an established causal effect.

| Domain | Questions to investigate | Measurement boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Player skill and experience | Repertoire, execution consistency, learning, specialization, dominant side, adaptation | Outcome residuals need shrinkage and opportunity adjustment; player identity is not a complete skill measurement |
| Biomechanics and equipment | Stance, joint/segment coordination, contact/release, swing or throw path, speed/spin, bat/racket/ball properties | Requires defined pose, motion, force or instrument measurements; action categories and court bearings are insufficient |
| Position and movement | Starting state, relative geometry, approach, recovery, spacing, velocity and acceleration | Snapshots, trajectories, orientation and gaze are distinct; frame alignment and visibility matter |
| Team tactics and coordination | Formations, roles, screens, pressing, blocking, passing networks, off-ball actions | Personnel or proximity alone does not establish assignments, communication or the intended play |
| Opponent response | Defensive pressure, matchup, anticipation, counterstrategy, repertoire interaction | Nearest opponent is not necessarily assigned opponent; adaptive responses complicate counterfactuals |
| Environment and venue | Surface, weather, altitude, lighting, dimensions, home context | Venue labels and historical averages cannot silently replace event-time environmental measurements |
| Workload, fatigue and recovery | Recent exposure, physical load, rest, travel, within-session changes | Workload is observable exposure; physiological fatigue and recovery require additional evidence |
| Injury and readiness | Verified availability, current limitation, return to play, measurement validity | Do not infer diagnosis or readiness from errors, pose appearance or reduced performance; no individualized medical tool is proposed |
| Game context and incentives | Score, count/down, time, pressure, phase, tournament situation | Reconstruct pre-event state; eventual result and hindsight-defined importance are not earlier information |
| Rules and officiating | Court/field rules, timing, scoring, equipment limits, zones and penalties | Date-stamped rule and measurement changes may require distinct eras; a changed field definition is not a player effect |
| Coaching and intention | Requested target, called action, practice intervention, feedback, tactical instruction | Actual intent or intervention must be recorded; executed placement does not reveal intended aim |
| Time and development | Aging, learning, evolving strategy, seasonality, provider and population change | Calendar order, information availability and within-session order answer different generalization questions |

## Candidate analysis structure

This is a prioritization under review, not a promise that every item will clear
the rights, measurement and sample-size gates. Each candidate has its own target
and population; the layers must not be pooled into a fictitious universal dataset.

| Sport | Tactical/event evidence under review | Mechanics companion under review | Principal missing evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Soccer | Shot geometry and recorded defensive context; geometry-only versus defensive-context ablation | Two-match broadcast-estimated 3-D body-pose sample, with joint-error flags and action alignment still under audit | Complete trajectories in event-only sources, intent, gaze, force, individualized readiness and causal tactical benefit |
| Basketball | Ten-game ACB at-release shot context, with source geometry/tracking consistency audit | Small nonprofessional free-throw pose dataset; bounded noncommercial source inspection under the additional eligibility conditions | Broad independent-game support, universal NBA transfer, in-game joint mechanics, complete possession-choice counterfactuals |
| Baseball | Count/sequence anticipation is reusable; measured pitch execution remains a separate Statcast rights/coverage decision | Laboratory pitching/hitting motion and force measurements; bounded noncommercial source inspection under the additional eligibility conditions | Permission for intended Statcast collection/publication, observed pitch intention, MLB-game joint mechanics and causal technique effects |
| American football | At-snap formation/personnel/motion and game-state anticipation; pressure risk only on an explicitly defined dropback cohort | Literature and a future tracking/pose measurement specification; no suitable unrestricted large mechanics corpus established | Leverage, trajectories, blocking assignments, called play/RPO intention, body mechanics and exact opponent response |
| Tennis | Charted serve/shot sequences and score context, with conservative grammar and match-level validation | Eight-player collegiate aiming experiment with stated targets, bounce coordinates and radar speed | Professional intent, continuous player/ball trajectories, spin/stance/joint mechanics, verified readiness and causal coaching effects |

## Why a small mechanics study can still be useful

A controlled or carefully documented practice dataset may observe a requested
target, body position or release condition that a much larger match archive omits.
It can support an auditable measurement demonstration and a bounded within-sample
or held-out-participant analysis. Its value does not require disguising five or
eight participants as hundreds of independent athletes because they repeated many
attempts. Success on practice trials cannot establish professional match impact.

Likewise, a broad event corpus can identify tendencies across many actual matches
without observing joint mechanics. Combining the two types of evidence in one
research project is useful; joining unrelated anonymous athletes or substituting
one population's labels for another is not.

## Prioritization rule before implementation

1. Complete the factor reviews and resolve important missing primary references
2. Establish source-use rights and verified units, timing, identity and quality
3. Prefer a question that directly uses recorded measurements and has a meaningful
   simple baseline, rather than choosing complexity for appearance
4. Decide whether the data support descriptive analysis, exploratory prediction,
   temporal evaluation, new-player evaluation or a genuine intervention study
5. Freeze feature groups and evaluation design before examining model performance
6. Build a clear analyst view showing context, comparable support, model reliability
   and missing information; add numerical coaching claims only if separately justified

## Open decisions

- Finish focused mechanics follow-up and final factor inventories for all sports
- Finish bounded laboratory source inspection under the independent,
  noncommercial project scope and confirmed absence of the excluded affiliations.
  Preserve the extra source restrictions; this does not authorize commercial use
- Complete soccer pose coverage/action alignment and basketball tracking quality
  rules before recommending a numerical angle or motion analysis
- Resolve the tennis experiment's exact serve-period semantics and censoring design
- Choose a defensible baseball first scope without claiming that outcome strings
  contain the requested physical pitch or bat measurements
- Have the independent methodological review challenge the proposed contracts

No single percentage can honestly summarize progress toward modelling “all
important factors.” The deliverable should instead make its evidence, usable
measurements, omissions, validation and next measurement needs inspectable.
