Project modifications include duplicate/conflict handling, match quarantine, score and serve-prefix parsing, a documented conservative 21-day availability assumption, history estimation and aggregate evaluation. Source dates and that lag do not establish verified completion or publication timestamps. Charted serve/rally direction categories are not observed ball coordinates, numerical angles or tracking.

This is an independent noncommercial research package. Raw charted points, row-level derivatives, individual predictions and fitted tactical weights are excluded from the controlled release. Source and transformation hashes remain available in local reproducibility records.
