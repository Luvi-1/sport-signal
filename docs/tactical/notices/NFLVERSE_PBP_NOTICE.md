# nflverse play-by-play attribution

Source: [nflverse data releases](https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data), play-by-play release. Credit the nflverse contributors and their documented underlying sources. The general data grant is [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), subject to release-specific exceptions; participation and FTN charting have their own notice in this directory.

The project filters regular-season eligible plays, reconstructs the declared final-at-snap context, and derives a realized-dropback target. Exact source URLs, retrieval times and SHA-256 hashes are bound in the local source/run manifests. Changes and cohort rules are documented in the football protocol and model card. Provider-derived post-snap quantities are excluded from model inputs.

No endorsement is implied. Public reports contain aggregate research; row-level play tables and fitted tactical weights are excluded from this release. The source license is not replaced by the repository's software license.
