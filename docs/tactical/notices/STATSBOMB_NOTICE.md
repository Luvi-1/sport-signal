This project uses it for independent noncommercial research. The agreement restricts commercial use of the data and derived analysis, prohibits third-party redistribution of the data, and requires StatsBomb attribution and logo in published analysis. Consult the agreement before any different use.

The project transforms event locations into shot geometry and partial recorded-defender summaries, applies documented eligibility checks and evaluates fixed statistical models. These transformations are the project's work; StatsBomb does not endorse the project. Native event coordinates are not presented as measured metres or complete tracking.

The controlled release includes credited aggregate research only. Raw event files, per-shot derived records, individual predictions and trained model weights remain excluded. The source-provided logo is retained for required attribution at `experiments/tactical/web/statsbomb-logo.png`; its presence does not grant general trademark or data rights.
